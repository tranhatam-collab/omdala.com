OMDALA Trust System

Trust scores are built from verifiable activity and are designed to be explainable, auditable, and operational.

OverviewPlatformAPI ReferenceTrustOnboarding

Signal Sources

  • Verification level and proof coverage
  • Completion and fulfillment quality
  • Behavioral moderation outcomes
  • Economic reliability and payment history
  • Governance actions and response discipline

Operating Rules

  • Every trust decision must point to evidence, not unexplained constants.
  • Critical moderation events can cap or reset trust bands immediately.
  • Product surfaces should expose trust status with action guidance, not just raw scores.
